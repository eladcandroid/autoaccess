# Wave: demo film storylines

The one thing: a driver who cannot speak or hear can now answer a message, start navigation
or decline a call while driving, with one hand and no voice.
What the viewer should remember: "Android Auto needs a voice. Wave needs a hand."
Spine: one driver, one incoming message ("Dana: when are you arriving?"), from the moment it
arrives to "sent".

Facts used on screen (all from the app as built): gestures are open palm, 1/2/3 fingers, fist,
nod, head shake; a pick is carried out after a 1.4 s ring; a fist held 3 s sends an emergency
alert; recognition runs on the phone. The app currently runs demo scenarios and sends nothing.

## A. The message (recommended)
Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: object-to-object chain.
A message lands while he drives. The car offers the only reply it knows, a voice, and he has
none. He lifts two fingers, a ring closes, and the reply is sent.
- First 2 s: aluminium ground, the message bubble "Dana: when are you arriving?" lands large,
  time stamp 08:42.
- Beats: message lands → "Reply by voice" fails, the line strikes out → title "Wave" →
  real screen recording: two fingers, tile inverts, ring closes → real result screen
  "sent" → three fast real results (route, declined call, music) → claim → end card.
- Look: Cockpit. Aluminium ground, ink bezel, Karantina titles, orange only on the ring,
  the payoff and the end card.
- Sound: none until the ring closes, then one soft tick and a low music bed to the end.

## B. No sound (the bold one)
Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on a visual beat.
The film is silent on purpose and says so. Giant Karantina lines and hand pictograms cut in
rhythm; the viewer experiences the drive the way the driver does.
- First 2 s: full-frame type "This film has no sound." on dial white.
- Beats: "This film has no sound" → "Neither does his car" → pictograms flash 1, 2, 3 →
  real screen recording of a pick → real result → claim → end card.
- Look: dial white and ink only, type at 70% of frame height, pictograms from the app.
- Sound: none at all, by design.

## C. Around the dial
Device: the object is the explanation. Form: one frame that rebuilds.
One instrument dial fills the frame. The needle sweeps to six stops; at each stop a gesture
pictogram appears with the real screen it triggers.
- First 2 s: the dial at full frame, needle sweeping from zero to the open palm.
- Beats: palm wakes → 1, 2, 3 pick → ring carries it out → fist cancels → long fist,
  emergency → nod and shake → end card inside the dial.
- Look: the app's gauge at giant scale, ink on aluminium, orange needle.
- Sound: a mechanical tick per stop over a quiet bed.
